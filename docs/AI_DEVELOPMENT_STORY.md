\# Building EvidenceHunter with AI Assistance



\## The Experiment



EvidenceHunter began as an experiment:



Can someone without formal programming training use AI tools to independently create, debug, document, and maintain a complex software project?



Instead of treating AI as a simple code generator, this project explores AI as:



\- a design partner

\- a debugging assistant

\- a documentation collaborator

\- a research workflow assistant



\---



\## Development Philosophy



The project emphasizes:



\- understanding before implementation

\- explicit assumptions

\- reproducible workflows

\- documented decisions

\- verification over confidence



AI-generated output is not accepted blindly.



Every important change requires review, testing, and validation.



\---



\## Why This Matters



Modern AI systems lower the barrier to software creation.



However, the challenge is no longer only writing code.



The challenge becomes:



\- asking the right questions

\- designing reliable systems

\- evaluating generated solutions

\- maintaining quality over time



EvidenceHunter explores this new development model.
